## Completion Rate Formula

```
Task completion rate (%) = (tasks completed without help) ÷ (total tasks attempted) × 100
```

Example: if 36 of 40 tasks (5 participants × 8 tasks) are completed without help:  
36 ÷ 40 × 100 = **90 %** — this meets the target.
