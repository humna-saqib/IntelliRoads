> **Instructions**
> - Add one row per distinct problem (not one row per participant who hit it).
> - "Users hit it" is the count out of 5 participants who encountered this problem.

> - "Commit ID" is the short Git SHA of the fix commit (leave blank until fixed).
> - "Re-tested" — yes only after at least one follow-up observation or cognitive walk-through.
