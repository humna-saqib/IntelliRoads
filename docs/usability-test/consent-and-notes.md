### What this study is about

We are evaluating the usability of a web-based traffic management dashboard. We want to understand how easy it is to find information and carry out tasks without technical training.

### What you will be asked to do

- Use the dashboard on a computer for approximately **30 minutes**.
- Talk aloud about what you see and what you are trying to do.
- Complete up to 8 short tasks and answer a brief questionnaire.

### Voluntary participation

Your participation is entirely **voluntary**. You may stop at any time and for any reason without any consequences. You do not need to explain why you wish to stop.

### Anonymity and data use

- You will be assigned a **participant code** (P1–P5). Your name will not be recorded anywhere.
- Notes and any screen recording will be used only for this study and will not be shared with third parties.
- Findings will be reported in aggregate (e.g. "3 out of 5 participants…"). No individual will be identifiable.
- Raw notes and recordings will be deleted after the study analysis is complete.

If recorded, the recording is used only to review task timings and interactions; it will not be shared publicly.
